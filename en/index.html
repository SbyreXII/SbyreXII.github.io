Integration · From XDE to Omniverse

XDE is the lab's physics engine: compared with PhysX, the engine bundled with Omniverse, it stands out for its highly accurate handling of multiple simultaneous contacts. Its features are developed by the team's mathematicians and physicists. My role: integrating them into Omniverse, following a structure set up as a team that lets us add new features without breaking those that already exist.
